# Android closed test: tester guide

Google needs **at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row**, actively using the app, before production access.

## What testers do
1. **Day 1:** open the opt-in link on their Android phone (same Gmail as on the tester list) → *Become a tester* → install Perkly from Google Play → sign up and finish the profile.
2. **Every day or two for 14 days:** browse Home / Explore / a category / a perk, use search and favourites, check Rewards (quests, streak, XP), spin the wheel, switch to Arabic once in Settings, check notifications and the Me tab.
3. **Once or twice:** scan the Perkly Test Café QR (`perkly-test-cafe-qr.pdf`, print it or show it on a screen), enter any amount, redeem. Try "At the Cashier" and "In the App" (test mode, no real charge). Optionally request a refund.
4. **Feedback:** Play Store → Perkly → *Send private feedback*, or a WhatsApp screenshot with one line.
5. **Don't** leave the test or uninstall before the 14 days are over.

The Test Café (`PK-9033BF28DA3DD23C`) is listed in `app_settings.test_merchants`, so it has no redemption limits. Remove it before launch (part of the launch cleanup).
